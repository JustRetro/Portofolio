Soft Skills

  1. Communication: Able to explain complex technical ideas in simple terms that clients and non-technical team members can understand.
  2. Teamwork: Able to collaborate effectively with others to achieve common goals.
  3. Problem-solving: Able to identify and resolve issues in a logical and efficient manner.
  4. Time management: Able to prioritize tasks and manage time effectively to meet deadlines.
  5. Adaptability: Able to adjust to new situations and changes in the work environment.
